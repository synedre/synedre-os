## Context

The situation that forced a decision. What forces are in tension? What is
constrained, and by what? State the problem, not the solution. A reader landing
here a year later must be able to reconstruct *why anyone had to choose at all*.

Include the relevant facts and the alternatives that were on the table. If a
constraint is external (a license, a law, a hard requirement), name it.

## Decision

The choice we made, stated in one or two crisp sentences up front, then elaborated.
"We will X." Not "we considered X" — the consideration is the Context and the
Alternatives. This section is the answer.
